Privacy Policy – NYC Health Grades Extension

This Chrome extension displays NYC Health Department restaurant letter grades (A, B, C) and inspection details on Google Maps.

Data Collection: The extension fetches public restaurant inspection data from the NYC Health Department and stores user preferences locally in Chrome storage.

Usage: Data is used solely to display restaurant grades and inspection details.

Third-party access: No data is shared with third parties.

User Control: Users can clear local storage at any time through Chrome settings.
